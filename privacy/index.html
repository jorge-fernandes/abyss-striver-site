Abyss Striver – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-11 · Version: 1 (offline build)

Abyss Striver ("the game") is developed and published by Jorge Fernandes, an individual developer based in the European Union ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game does with your data. The short version: the current release collects nothing and sends nothing.

1. Data we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no network access. The Android package requests no permissions.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally, in the app's private storage on your device:

  • the current run (your hero, inventory and dungeon);
  • the Hall of the Fallen (names you give your heroes, their depth, level and cause of death);
  • achievements, the monster journal, daily-run results and settings (language, volumes).

These files never leave your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them. On Android, the game opts out of system backups (allowBackup=false), so they are not copied to Google's backup service either. The hero names you type are only shown back to you; please avoid entering other people's personal information.

3. Children

The game is not directed at children under 16 and contains fantasy violence. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone; see section 1.

4. Third parties

The game contains no third-party SDKs. It is built with Godot Engine and uses open-licensed art, music and fonts; their notices are listed in the game under Credits → Licences.

5. Future features

We plan to add optional features that would process some data: a leaderboard through Google Play Games Services, ads through Google AdMob (with consent asked first where the law requires it), and an in-app purchase through Google Play Billing. None of these is in the current release. Before any of them ships, this policy will be updated with what each one collects and why, and the in-app copy and the store listing will link to the new version.

6. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. If you believe the game has stored something it should not have, contact us and we will help.

7. Changes

We will post changes to this page and update the effective date above. Material changes will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.
